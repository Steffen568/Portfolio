Dry-eye clinic

Dry, burning or watery eyes

Do your eyes feel like this?

Any of these can be a sign of dry eye.

  • Sore, tired eyes
  • Blurry vision that comes and goes
  • Feels like something’s in your eye
  • Burning or stinging
  • Red eyes
  • Watery eyesWatery eyes are often a sign of dry eye.
