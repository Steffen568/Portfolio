Kids’ eyes and hard-to-fit contacts

Both are Dr. A’s specialties: Myopia and specialty contact lenses. [CONFIRM]

Kids

Signs a child may need an eye exam:

  • Squint to see the board or TV
  • Sit very close to screens
  • Complain of headaches or tired eyes
  • Avoid distance activities
  • Have frequent prescription changes

The options: Specialized contact lenses: Orthokeratology and multifocal lenses; Eyeglasses with a special lens design; Atropine drops; Low Level Light Therapy (LLLT). [CONFIRM with the doctors]
