Questions families ask

Before you call.

Is there an application fee?
No. Applying is free. After acceptance there's a $25 enrollment fee.
Can we use TEFA at Holy Cross?
Yes. For 2026–27, TEFA provided up to $10,474 per student at a private school. Holy Cross asks every family to apply. See what it does to your cost.
Which grades do you teach?
Grades 6 through 12, co-ed, so a student can start in middle school and graduate from Holy Cross.
What does tuition include?
Instruction, technology including a Chromebook, e-books where used, athletics, extracurriculars, fine arts and graduation. Uniforms, meals and the photo package are extra. There are no mandatory fundraisers.
How do payments work?
Through FACTS, on an 11-month, 10-month, twice-monthly, every-two-weeks or twice-a-year plan, or in full by August for 3% off. Credit-card payments carry a 2.95% fee.
Do you offer college credit?
Yes, dual-credit courses at $250 per course, books extra. CONFIRM university partner

About this build

Phase 1 is a new public front door for the school: Home, Your real cost, Admissions, Outcomes, Contact and Español. A parent on a phone, in English or Spanish, can learn the real cost, see the college result, and ask, visit or apply. The school's existing systems stay underneath, and nothing is rebuilt.

Phase 2 holds safety, our story, academics and student life.

Built for the school as a concept. Pending board approval.
