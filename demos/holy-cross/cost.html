How this estimate works
  1. Order: tuition → percentage discount (service) → dollar discounts (alumni, sibling) → scholarships → TEFA → Hope for the Future → 426 Tuition Assistance → referral credits → 3% paid-in-full. CONFIRM order with business office
  2. Sibling discount: $500 for each child after the first. The first child entered is treated as the first child.
  3. TEFA pays toward tuition after discounts and never more than tuition. Anything left can go toward other qualified expenses such as uniforms, books, school meals or transportation (Texas Comptroller). CONFIRM how leftover funds are handled
  4. Hope for the Future isn't applied to a child who has TEFA; the school says those funds are reallocated to families without TEFA.
  5. 426 Tuition Assistance requires TEFA and Hope for the Future decisions first, and can't be combined with the service discount or referral credits.
  6. Scholarships for incoming 9th graders: Valedictorian/Salutatorian $1,000 a year (renewable with a 3.5 GPA) and the New Freshman Scholarship, $500–$2,000, one year only.
  7. Not included: memorial scholarships, Catholic Life Sweepstakes credits and outside scholarships, which vary by family.

Sources: Holy Cross 2026–27 Tuition & Fees and Scholarships & Tuition Assistance documents; Holy Cross Financial Support page; Texas Comptroller, Texas Education Freedom Accounts.

About this build

Phase 1 is a new public front door for the school: Home, Your real cost, Admissions, Outcomes, Contact and Español. A parent on a phone, in English or Spanish, can learn the real cost, see the college result, and ask, visit or apply. The school's existing systems stay underneath, and nothing is rebuilt.

Phase 2 holds safety, our story, academics and student life.
